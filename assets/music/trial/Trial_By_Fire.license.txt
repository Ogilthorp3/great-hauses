THE BRIEF: "medieval music meets Kraftwerk" — D Dorian modal melody on
lute / recorder / hurdy-gurdy timbres over an analog motorik sequencer, with
the last four bars flattening the 2nd to E-flat (D Phrygian) for the
turnaround. 128 BPM at 48 kHz is 22 500 samples per beat EXACTLY, so 32 bars
is 60.000000 s and the loop seam is not a click.

WHY THREE FILES AND NOT FIVE STEMS: the three tiers are the SAME 60 s loop
rendered with progressively more layers switched on —
  tier1 = sequencer pulse                       (the fuse)
  tier2 = + kick, tabor, hats, bass             (the kegs)
  tier3 = + lute, recorder, hurdy-gurdy, arp    (the dragon)
Because they are the same loop, MusicManager plays all three AT ONCE, in
sample-lock, and only crossfades their volumes (see `trial_tier()`). That is
real layering, not a track change: nothing re-seeks, so a tier change never
drops a beat and the phase can never drift.
