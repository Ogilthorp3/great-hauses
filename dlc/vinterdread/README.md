# Haus Vinterdread — Viking Mythology & Wendigo Dread-Wizard DLC

> *"Winter is the final boss. We are its heralds."*

Welcome to **Haus Vinterdread**, the premier expansion pack for **Great Hauses**. Rooted in authentic Norse mythology and dark-fantasy Canadian winter folklore, Haus Vinterdread fields a terrifying, frost-forged army led by the legendary **Wendigo Dread-Wizard**.

---

## 🏔️ The Lore of the North

Born in the perpetual whiteout of **Jötunheim Keep**, Haus Vinterdread represents the indomitable spirit of the northern frost. Where lesser kings wear crowns of polished gold, the Allfather Sorcerer of Vinterdread crowns his dreadlocks with **ancient deer antlers** and wields the frost-carved staff of Yggdrasil.

## 🚀 How to Install & Play

### Option 1: Automatic User Install
Copy the `vinterdread` folder into your Great Hauses user directory:
- **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Great Hauses/hauses/vinterdread/`
- **Linux:** `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Great Hauses/hauses/vinterdread/`
- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Great Hauses\hauses\vinterdread\`

### Option 2: Standalone Validation
Run the Great Hauses validator tool:
```bash
Godot --headless -s res://tools/validate_house_pack.gd -- dlc/vinterdread
```

Once installed, launch the game. **Haus Vinterdread** will automatically hang its frost-carved banner in the **Hall of Banners**!
